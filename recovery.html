ADDICTION RECOVERY

RECOVERY.

Recovery can look different for different people. It can include treatment, peer support, counseling, medication, harm-reduction tools, rebuilding routines, and learning how to handle cravings without returning to substance use.

Cravings

Cravings can be intense, but they pass. Learning triggers, delaying action, changing surroundings, contacting support, and using a recovery plan can help someone get through them.

Relapse

A relapse does not erase the value of previous recovery time. Returning to support quickly can reduce harm and help someone restart recovery.

Support programs

Peer-support programs such as Narcotics Anonymous, SMART Recovery, Recovery Dharma, and LifeRing are different approaches people may use alongside professional treatment or on their own.

Treatment

Treatment may include counseling, outpatient or residential care, and medications for some substance-use disorders. The best approach depends on the person, substance, health needs, and circumstances.

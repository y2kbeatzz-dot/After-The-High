CANNABIS / WEED AWARENESS

CANNABIS.

Cannabis can affect attention, short-term memory, coordination, reaction time, mood, and judgment. Higher-THC products can produce stronger effects than some people expect.

Weed / flower

THC can impair memory, coordination, and reaction time. Some people experience anxiety, panic, paranoia, or confusion.

Edibles

Edibles can take longer to feel and may last much longer than inhaled cannabis. That delay can lead people to take more before the first dose has fully taken effect.

Dabs and concentrates

Concentrates can contain much more THC than traditional flower. Higher exposure can increase the chance of panic, severe intoxication, vomiting, or psychotic-like symptoms in some people.

Dependence and CHS

Some people develop tolerance, cravings, and difficulty cutting down. Long-term frequent cannabis use can also be linked to cannabinoid hyperemesis syndrome, which causes repeated severe nausea and vomiting in some people.

Driving and mixing

Driving while high is unsafe because cannabis can affect attention, coordination, and reaction time. Mixing cannabis with alcohol or other drugs can increase impairment and unpredictability.

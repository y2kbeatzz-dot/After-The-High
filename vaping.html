VAPING AWARENESS

VAPING.

Vapes can deliver nicotine quickly and can expose the lungs to aerosol containing ultrafine particles, flavoring chemicals, and other substances.

Nicotine addiction

Repeated nicotine use can build dependence. Cravings, irritability, anxiety, and trouble concentrating can happen when someone cuts back or stops.

Not just water vapor

Vape aerosol is not simply water. Its contents vary by product and can include nicotine and other chemicals or particles.

Youth and the developing brain

Nicotine can affect brain systems involved in attention, learning, mood, and impulse control.

THC vapes

Some vape devices contain THC or other substances. Potency and contents may be difficult to judge, especially in unregulated products.

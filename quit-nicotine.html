NICOTINE REPLACEMENT & QUIT TOOLS

TOOLS TO HELP
BREAK THE LOOP.

Nicotine replacement therapy (NRT) gives the body nicotine without cigarette smoke or vape aerosol. It is designed to reduce withdrawal symptoms and cravings while someone works on changing the routines and triggers connected to nicotine use.

STEADY SUPPORTNicotine Patch

Worn on the skin and releases nicotine slowly over many hours. It can help with background cravings through the day.

FAST CRAVING HELPNicotine Gum

Provides nicotine through the lining of the mouth. It can be used when a craving hits rather than giving nicotine continuously.

DISSOLVES SLOWLYNicotine Lozenge

Dissolves in the mouth and can help with sudden cravings. It works similarly to gum without chewing.

PRESCRIPTION OPTIONNicotine Inhaler

A prescription quit aid in the U.S. that delivers nicotine through the mouth and may also help replace part of the hand-to-mouth routine.

PRESCRIPTION OPTIONNicotine Nasal Spray

A fast-acting prescription form of nicotine replacement that can help with strong cravings.

COMBINATION NRTPatch + Gum/Lozenge

Some adults use a patch for steady support with gum or a lozenge for breakthrough cravings. A clinician or pharmacist can help choose an approach.

Important: nicotine replacement still contains nicotine and should be used as directed. Teens, people who are pregnant, people with certain heart conditions, or anyone unsure which option fits them should ask a clinician or pharmacist. Counseling and quit support can also improve the chance of staying nicotine-free.

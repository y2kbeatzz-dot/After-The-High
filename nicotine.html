NICOTINE AWARENESS

NICOTINE.

Nicotine is an addictive drug found in cigarettes, many vapes, and nicotine pouches. Dependence can build through repeated use.

Cravings and withdrawal

People who are dependent on nicotine may experience cravings, irritability, restlessness, anxiety, or difficulty concentrating when they stop or cut back.

Vapes and nicotine pouches

These products can deliver nicotine without cigarette smoke, but they can still create dependence and withdrawal.

Nicotine replacement therapy

Nicotine patches, gum, and lozenges are designed as quit aids. They provide measured nicotine without cigarette smoke or vape aerosol and can help reduce withdrawal while someone changes the habit itself.

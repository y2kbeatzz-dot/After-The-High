Know what you're facing.

Drugs can affect breathing, heart rhythm, temperature, judgment and consciousness. Unknown contents and mixing substances can make outcomes harder to predict.

Fentanyl

Illicit fentanyl can appear in counterfeit pills and other drugs without someone knowing. Opioid overdose can slow or stop breathing.

M30

Cocaine

Cocaine can sharply raise heart rate and blood pressure and contribute to arrhythmias, seizures, stroke or heart attack.

Meth

Methamphetamine can dangerously increase heart rate, blood pressure and body temperature and can contribute to psychosis or seizures.

Heroin & opioids

Opioids can suppress the brain's drive to breathe. Heroin, fentanyl and misused prescription opioids can cause fatal overdose.

Lean / codeine

Codeine is an opioid. Combining it with alcohol, benzodiazepines or other depressants can dangerously slow breathing.

Xanax / benzos

Benzodiazepines can cause heavy sedation and impaired coordination. Mixing them with opioids or alcohol raises overdose risk.

MDMA

MDMA can raise body temperature and heart rate and affect fluid balance. Products sold as ecstasy or molly may contain unexpected substances.

♥

Ketamine

Ketamine can cause dissociation, impaired awareness and loss of coordination.

Weed / THC

Cannabis can affect memory, coordination, reaction time, mood and judgment. High-THC products may trigger panic, paranoia or severe confusion in some people.

☘

Nicotine / Vaping

Nicotine is highly addictive. Vapes can expose the lungs to nicotine, ultrafine particles and other substances. Cravings and withdrawal can make quitting difficult.

Nicotine pouches

Nicotine pouches avoid smoke, but they still contain addictive nicotine and can build dependence, cravings and withdrawal.

NICOTINE

Alcohol

Alcohol poisoning can suppress breathing and consciousness. Combining it with opioids or sedatives can be especially dangerous.

Unknown pills

Counterfeit pills can look like legitimate medication while containing fentanyl or other unexpected drugs.

?

Overdose signs

Possible opioid overdose signs include inability to wake, slow or stopped breathing, choking or gurgling sounds, and discolored lips or fingertips.

CHECK BREATHING

Naloxone saves lives

Naloxone can reverse an opioid overdose. Give it if available, call 911, and stay with the person until help arrives.

NALOXONE

Mixing changes everything.

Using multiple substances can make effects unpredictable. Stimulants do not cancel opioid overdose risk.

MY STORY · CRYSTAL

I'VE BEEN
DRUGS FREE FOR

1 YEAR SOBER

My story is bigger than addiction, but this part is about what drugs became for me — escape, dependence, and something I had to fight to get away from.

For a long time, using was not just about wanting to get high. It became a way to quiet everything for a while. The problem was that the quiet never lasted. When the high wore off, whatever I was trying not to feel was still there, and addiction kept asking for more.

There is a point where something that once felt like an escape starts making your world smaller. Your choices start revolving around the next time you can use, feel numb, or avoid feeling sick, anxious, empty, or overwhelmed.

Getting sober meant losing the shortcut I used to escape. I had to learn that cravings are feelings, not commands, and that one terrible night does not have to become a relapse.

Some days sobriety feels powerful. Other days it is quiet and ordinary: making it through the day, going to sleep sober, waking up and doing it again. Those ordinary days matter too.

I still count the time because the number means something to me. It is proof that I have kept choosing my life over the thing that was taking it away from me.

VAPING & NICOTINE AWARENESS

VAPING ISN'T
JUST WATER VAPOR.

Most vapes contain nicotine, which is highly addictive. Vape aerosol can contain nicotine, ultrafine particles, flavoring chemicals and other substances. This section is for teens, parents, educators and adults trying to quit — without shame.

DEPENDENCE

Cravings & withdrawal

Repeated nicotine use can make the brain and body expect it, causing cravings, irritability and trouble concentrating when someone stops.

YOUTH

Developing brain

Nicotine can affect brain systems involved in attention, learning, mood and impulse control.

AEROSOL

Not just water vapor

Vape aerosol can contain nicotine, particles, flavoring chemicals and other substances.

THC VAPES

Different substance, similar device

Some vapes contain THC or other substances. Potency and contents may be difficult to judge.

NICOTINE POUCHES

No smoke does not mean no addiction

Pouches can still deliver nicotine and create dependence, cravings and withdrawal.

QUITTING

Support can help

Quitlines, counseling, nicotine replacement and healthcare support can make quitting more manageable.

NICOTINE REPLACEMENT & QUIT TOOLS

TOOLS TO HELP
BREAK THE LOOP.

Nicotine replacement therapy (NRT) gives the body nicotine without cigarette smoke or vape aerosol. The goal is to reduce withdrawal and cravings while someone changes the habit and routines connected to nicotine use.

STEADY SUPPORTNicotine Patch

Worn on the skin and releases nicotine slowly over many hours for background cravings.

FAST CRAVING HELPNicotine Gum

Provides nicotine through the mouth and can be useful when a craving hits.

DISSOLVES SLOWLYNicotine Lozenge

Dissolves in the mouth and can help with sudden cravings without chewing.

PRESCRIPTION OPTIONNicotine Inhaler

A prescription quit aid in the U.S. that delivers nicotine through the mouth.

PRESCRIPTION OPTIONNicotine Nasal Spray

A fast-acting prescription form of nicotine replacement for strong cravings.

COMBINATION NRTPatch + Gum/Lozenge

Some adults use a patch for steady support plus gum or a lozenge for breakthrough cravings.

Important: nicotine replacement still contains nicotine and should be used as directed. Teens, people who are pregnant, people with certain heart conditions, or anyone unsure which option fits them should ask a clinician or pharmacist.

COMMON QUESTIONS

FAQ.

Can someone become addicted to vaping?

Yes. Nicotine is highly addictive, and regular use can lead to cravings, withdrawal symptoms and difficulty stopping.

What is nicotine replacement therapy?

NRT uses measured nicotine from products such as patches, gum or lozenges to reduce withdrawal without cigarette smoke or vape aerosol.

Are nicotine pouches the same as quit aids?

No. Recreational nicotine pouches are not the same thing as approved nicotine-replacement products designed and labeled for quitting.

What does naloxone do?

Naloxone can temporarily reverse an opioid overdose. Call 911 even after giving it.

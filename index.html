Know what
you're facing.

Drugs can affect breathing, heart rhythm, temperature, judgment and consciousness. Unknown contents and mixing substances can make the outcome harder to predict.

Fentanyl

Illicit fentanyl is extremely potent and can appear in counterfeit pills and other illegal drugs without someone knowing. Opioid overdose can slow or stop breathing.

M30

Cocaine

Cocaine is a powerful stimulant that can sharply raise heart rate and blood pressure and contribute to arrhythmias, seizures, stroke or heart attack.

Meth

Methamphetamine can dangerously increase heart rate, blood pressure and body temperature. Severe effects can include agitation, psychosis, seizures and cardiovascular emergencies.

Heroin & opioids

Opioids can suppress the brain's drive to breathe. Heroin, fentanyl and misused prescription opioids can cause fatal overdose, especially when combined with other depressants.

Lean / codeine

Codeine is an opioid. Combining opioids with alcohol, benzodiazepines or other depressants can dangerously slow breathing.

Xanax / benzos

Benzodiazepines can cause heavy sedation and impaired coordination. Combining them with opioids or alcohol increases overdose risk.

MDMA

MDMA can raise body temperature and heart rate and affect fluid balance. Products sold as ecstasy or molly may contain unexpected substances.

♥

Ketamine

Ketamine can cause dissociation, impaired awareness and loss of coordination. High exposure or mixing with other depressants can increase danger.

Alcohol

Alcohol is a central nervous system depressant. Alcohol poisoning can suppress breathing and consciousness. Combining it with opioids or sedatives can be especially dangerous.

Unknown pills

Counterfeit pills can look like legitimate medication while containing fentanyl or other unexpected drugs. Appearance alone cannot tell you what is inside.

?

Overdose signs

Possible opioid overdose signs include inability to wake, slow or stopped breathing, choking or gurgling sounds, and discolored lips or fingertips.

CHECK BREATHING

Naloxone saves lives

Naloxone can reverse an opioid overdose. Give it if available, call 911, support breathing when appropriate, and stay with the person until help arrives.

NALOXONE

Mixing changes everything.

Using multiple substances can make effects unpredictable. Stimulants do not cancel opioid overdose risk, and depressants can compound breathing suppression.

DAYS SOBER.

My story is bigger than addiction, but this part of the site is about the part drugs played in it — the escape, the dependence, and what it has taken to stay sober.

“Sobriety as Sabotage.”

In the story I wrote, I described reaching eight months without a drop. That was one point in the journey. I kept going.

Addiction can turn escape into a trap. It can take over your time, your choices, your relationships, your health, and your sense of who you are. Getting sober did not erase everything overnight. It meant learning how to live without reaching for a substance every time life hurt.

Recovery has meant surviving cravings, choosing not to use on bad days, starting over mentally without starting over physically, and understanding that sobriety is something I protect one day at a time.

Today I am 504 days sober. I put my story here because addiction is not just a statistic and recovery is not just a number. There is a real person behind every one of those days.

No single page can literally list every psychoactive chemical, prescription medicine, analog, or emerging street drug. This index expands the site across the major drug families and commonly encountered substances, with the focus on risks rather than how to use them.

OPIOIDHeroin

Can slow or stop breathing and cause fatal overdose.

OPIOIDFentanyl

Extremely potent opioid strongly linked to fatal overdose and counterfeit pills.

OPIOIDCarfentanil

An exceptionally potent synthetic opioid; exposure can be life-threatening.

OPIOIDOxycodone / Percocet

Prescription opioids can cause dependence and respiratory depression when misused.

OPIOIDHydrocodone / Vicodin

Can cause dependence, sedation and overdose, especially with other depressants.

OPIOIDCodeine / Lean

Codeine is an opioid and can suppress breathing, especially in mixtures.

OPIOIDMorphine

Powerful opioid that can cause dangerous respiratory depression when misused.

OPIOIDTramadol

Opioid pain medicine; misuse can cause overdose and seizures.

OPIOIDMethadone

Long-acting opioid that can cause fatal overdose if used outside medical guidance.

STIMULANTCocaine / Crack

Can cause arrhythmia, seizure, stroke, heart attack and addiction.

STIMULANTMethamphetamine / Crystal Meth

Can cause severe cardiovascular stress, overheating, psychosis and addiction.

STIMULANTAmphetamine / Adderall misuse

Misuse can raise heart rate, blood pressure and body temperature.

STIMULANTMethylphenidate / Ritalin misuse

Misuse may cause cardiovascular problems, agitation and dependence.

STIMULANTSynthetic cathinones / “Bath salts”

Can cause severe agitation, paranoia, overheating and dangerous cardiovascular effects.

DEPRESSANTAlcohol

Can cause poisoning, loss of consciousness and slowed breathing.

BENZODIAZEPINEAlprazolam / Xanax

Can cause dependence and dangerous sedation, especially with opioids or alcohol.

BENZODIAZEPINEDiazepam / Valium

Can impair coordination and breathing when combined with other depressants.

BENZODIAZEPINEClonazepam / Klonopin

Dependence and withdrawal can be serious; mixtures with opioids raise overdose risk.

SEDATIVEBarbiturates

Can severely depress the central nervous system and cause fatal overdose.

SEDATIVEZolpidem / Ambien misuse

Can cause heavy sedation, memory problems and dangerous impairment.

DISSOCIATIVEPCP

DISSOCIATIVEDXM

PSYCHEDELICPsilocybin mushrooms

Can alter perception and judgment and trigger panic or confusion.

PSYCHEDELICDMT

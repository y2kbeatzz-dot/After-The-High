FENTANYL AWARENESS

FENTANYL.

Fentanyl is a very potent synthetic opioid. Illicit fentanyl can be present in counterfeit pills and other illegal drugs without a person knowing, which can make overdose risk difficult to predict.

Why fentanyl is dangerous

Like other opioids, fentanyl can slow the brain's drive to breathe. Too much can cause extreme sleepiness, unconsciousness, slowed or stopped breathing, and death.

Possible opioid overdose signs

A person may be difficult or impossible to wake, breathing may be very slow or stopped, and they may make choking or gurgling sounds. Skin, lips, or fingertips can become pale, gray, or blue depending on skin tone.

Naloxone

Naloxone can temporarily reverse an opioid overdose. If an overdose is suspected, give naloxone if available, call 911, and stay with the person until emergency help arrives.

Counterfeit pills

Illegal counterfeit pills can resemble legitimate prescription medications while containing fentanyl or other unexpected substances. Appearance alone cannot confirm what is inside.
